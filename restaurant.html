Welcome to kubaba Mandi

Since its inception in 2013, kubaba Mandi has swiftly risen to prominence
as a leading Mandi Restaurant, specializing in Authentic Arabian cuisine
in the vibrant state of Kerala, India. Our remarkable journey is a testament to our
unwavering determination, boundless passion, and distinctive culinary
expertise that sets us apart. Indulge in the finest dining experience as
kubaba Mandi invites you to savor the essence of Arabian cuisine, where
passion meets precision, and each dish is a masterpiece in itself.
